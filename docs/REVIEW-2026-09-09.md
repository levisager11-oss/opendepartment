# Review — 9 September 2026

A second pass over the whole app, taking [AUDIT.md](../AUDIT.md) as read. That
audit's findings are not repeated here; this covers what it did not reach. The
baseline was commit `43ecb85`.

Seven findings were fixed in the same change as this document. The rest are
recorded rather than fixed, with what each would take.

## Fixed

| Finding | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Anyone holding a department's public URL and anon key could register a second slug against the same project — a live mirror with its own directory entry, and a way past suspension, since `status` is a flag on one row. | `departments_one_project` trigger and exclusive index in [db/control-plane.sql](../db/control-plane.sql) |
| Storage returns the Content-Type the uploader supplied, so an object filed as `application/pdf` could be stored as `text/html` and executed by the `<object>` element in a nested browsing context on the tenant's own supabase.co origin. | Sandboxed `<iframe>` in [FileViewer.tsx](../src/components/dept/FileViewer.tsx); `object-src 'none'` in [middleware.ts](../src/middleware.ts) |
| Nothing anywhere signed a person out of their OpenDepartment account — the session that registers departments and can authorise a project deletion. | [AccountSignOut.tsx](../src/components/account/AccountSignOut.tsx) |
| Signed exhibit URLs lived an hour and cannot be revoked, so deletion and banning both lagged by up to that long. | `SIGNED_URL_TTL` in [types.ts](../src/lib/tenant/types.ts) |
| The Ko-fi widget script loaded on marketing pages before any consent, contradicting the cookie notice's own "no third-party tag", and its `getHTML()` went into `innerHTML`. | [KofiButton.tsx](../src/components/KofiButton.tsx), privacy copy in [legal.ts](../src/lib/legal.ts) |
| Two administrators banning each other concurrently could leave a department with nobody who can moderate it, unrecoverable without the project owner's SQL editor. | Settings lock and outcome check in `admin_set_flag` |
| No member could leave a department or erase what they had filed, and no operator could sign out. | `leave_department()` and `/d/<slug>/account` |

Departments also record which version of `db/tenant-schema.sql` they have
actually run, in a `public.schema_version` row stamped by the last statement in
that file. Until now nothing could tell: the app discovered an out-of-date
department one missing function at a time, by the error, while a corrected
policy — the kind of change the file is versioned for — produced no symptom at
all. Administrators now get a strip under the header, the current SQL on the
administration screen, and a badge on the operator's account page.

A founder signup with a mistyped address claims the department and consumes the
verifier before the address is ever confirmed, and the reissue SQL is guarded on
`not claimed` so it cannot recover it. That one needs the project owner's own
SQL editor by design; the recovery is now written down in
[AUDIT-BOOTSTRAP.md](AUDIT-BOOTSTRAP.md).

## Open

### Storage admission: what is left of it

The quota is measured now, so what remains is narrower than the original
audit's finding:

- **Objects uploaded without being filed as a document** are counted by
  nothing, because there is no insert to intercept. `admin_orphaned_objects()`
  lists them an hour after upload, which is the only answer available: a
  storage policy bounds *where* a member may write and cannot express a running
  total.
- **The object's Content-Type is still client-supplied.** The sandboxed viewer
  makes that stop mattering for rendering. A storage INSERT policy testing
  `metadata->>'mimetype'` may be able to fence the value itself; whether
  `metadata` is populated at the point RLS runs was not established here, and a
  policy that is wrong about that refuses every upload.
- **Signed URLs expire in ten minutes rather than an hour**, which shortens the
  window after a deletion or a ban without closing it. Only a shorter TTL or a
  proxy closes it, and a proxy contradicts the property the README sells.

### Administration has no pagination

The lists now say when they are showing a subset, which was the misleading
half. They still cannot show the rest: files cap at 500, reports at 200 each
and the audit log at 100, and reading past that means the Supabase dashboard.
The audit log is the one that matters — it is the accountability record and
there is no export.

### Smaller

- The file page mints two signed URLs per view, one of them for a download link
  most visitors never click.
- The signup form's fallback error path still surfaces the raw Supabase
  message when nothing in `mapAuthError` recognises it.

## Second pass — fixed since

Schema version 2, and the first real exercise of the version notice added in
version 1.

- **The vault scales now.** A generated `tsvector` column with a GIN index
  (`to_tsvector('simple', …)`, no extension needed) replaces three `ilike
  '%…%'` scans, and `subject_ids` on the view replaces the id list the browser
  used to fetch and send back. The search term is tokenised on an allowlist
  into a prefix tsquery, so it is a filter *value* rather than syntax spliced
  into a PostgREST `or=(…)` grammar — and a half-typed word still matches,
  which `websearch_to_tsquery` alone would have broken. Filenames are split on
  `._-` first: Postgres reads `budget.pdf` as one token, so without that a
  document was not reachable by either word in its own name.
- **Member addresses stay current.** A trigger on `auth.users` follows a
  confirmed email change into `user_emails`. It was written once at signup and
  never again, so the address an administrator would use to contact somebody
  went stale silently.
- **The platform has an imprint**, at `/legal/imprint`, in both locales, naming
  where a complaint about a *department* goes instead.
- **Truncated admin lists say so.** A list that came back exactly at its limit
  now carries a notice on its tab, and the audit log exports as CSV — every
  field quoted, formula-injection guarded, BOM for Excel. Until now the only
  way to keep any of the accountability record was a screenshot, and
  `purge_department()` empties the table.
- **Signup asks for the password twice** and offers to show it. The way back
  from one typed wrong is a reset mail to a department you have not joined
  yet — and for a founder, one that does not exist yet.

## Third pass — fixed since

Schema version 3, plus control-plane changes.

- **Thumbnails.** A ~400px WebP made in the browser at upload and stored beside
  the original, so a 24-card page stops downloading every full-resolution scan
  out of the department owner's free tier. `thumb_path` is threaded through
  `delete_file()`, `purge_department()`, `leave_department()` and the orphan
  sweep, so a thumbnail is never left behind and never offered for deletion
  while its exhibit is live. `delete_file()` changed shape to return both
  paths; the app reads the old shape too, because it deploys before any
  administrator re-runs the file. Generating one is allowed to fail at every
  step — a card with no thumbnail falls back to the original.
- **The upload form says what it did not clean.** Only images are scrubbed, and
  silence about a video read as reassurance. PDFs and recordings now get a
  notice naming what travels with them.
- **The abuse queue has a reader.** `staff_list_reports()`,
  `staff_resolve_report()` and `staff_set_department_status()`, gated on
  `operators.is_staff` — a column outside every UPDATE grant, set by hand in
  the control plane's SQL editor. Suspension was previously only reachable by
  writing SQL, which meant the answer to a complaint depended on somebody
  having the right tab open. Deliberately not the env-var allowlist that was
  asked for: an env var can gate a screen, but the reports are one PostgREST
  call away, and this repo's own rule is that a check the app performs is
  decoration unless the database performs it too.
- **Unlisted says what it means.** The wizard and the directory now say that an
  unlisted address is not a secret one, since `slug_available()` has to answer
  before anybody has an account. The invite code and the department's own RLS
  are the lock; the address never was.

## Fourth pass — fixed since

Schema version 4.

- **`size_bytes` is measured, not claimed.** The object is uploaded before the
  row that describes it, so by the time the quota trigger runs, storage knows
  the real byte count. Reading it back turns every number drawn from that
  column — the cap, the storage meter, the per-member breakdown — from
  something members could choose into something measured. The lookup is
  wrapped, like every other reach into `storage.objects` here: a project where
  the schema's owner cannot read it falls back to the claimed value.
- **The test suite can isolate.** Each row of the runner's list gets its own
  database; `06-tenant-isolated.sql` is a third one, for assertions about
  totals or about who holds a role. Both times an assertion in this repo was
  written wrong, the cause was a shared fixture — a filled report queue, or a
  demoted administrator — rather than the code under test.
- **`verified_at` is gone.** It claimed the directory confirmed a department's
  schema and nothing ever wrote it. The honest version is a liveness timestamp
  something acts on; re-add it with that code.
- **Unrecognised auth errors** no longer print Supabase's own wording to
  somebody trying to join an archive. It goes to the console instead.
- **The operator contact field says it is public**, since it renders on an
  imprint that by definition has no login in front of it.

## Fifth pass — 28 September 2026

Schema version 5.

- **PDF previews were blank in every Chromium browser.** The sandboxed
  `<iframe>` from the first pass above is not allowed to load plugins, and
  Chromium's PDF viewer is one: Chrome and Edge showed a grey "blocked" page
  for every PDF exhibit. The viewer now fetches the bytes, refuses anything
  that does not open with `%PDF-`, and frames them as a Blob it typed
  `application/pdf` itself, so the uploader's Content-Type still never reaches
  a frame. `frame-src` is narrowed to `'self' blob:` to match. Checked in
  Chromium: sandboxed frame blocked, typed blob renders.
- **Leaving did not empty the bucket.** `leave_department()` hands the storage
  paths back after deleting the caller's profile, and the Storage API removes
  by name — a `DELETE … WHERE`, which Postgres checks against the SELECT
  policy too. That policy required an active member, so the removal matched
  nothing and reported success, and the documents the member had just
  "erased" stayed readable by every member. A caller with no profile may now
  read and delete their own folder; a banned member still may not, and both
  forms count what storage says it removed instead of trusting an empty
  success.
- **A banned member could still bulk-delete their comments and objects.** A
  `DELETE` with no `WHERE` reads no column, so only the DELETE policy applied,
  and neither asked about the ban. Both do now.
- **Dates hydrated as a mismatch** for anybody outside UTC: the server
  formatted in its own zone, the browser in the reader's. The first pass now
  formats in UTC on both sides and switches after hydration.
- **Signup refused anyone who pressed "Show password" first** — the repeat
  field was hidden but still compared.
- **Dark accent colours** put navy text on a navy call to action. Text on the
  accent is now navy or white by contrast, and the settings screen warns when
  the accent will be hard to read on the masthead.
- **A failed "load more"** replaced the whole vault with an error and retried
  from page one; it now keeps the loaded cards and retries the failed page.
- Next.js 15.5.26 (critical advisories in earlier 15.x), pluralised card
  counters, `aria-current` on the desktop nav, a translated menu label, card
  titles as `h2` under the page's `h1`, and a sitemap that no longer claims
  every static page changed at generation time.

### Decisions taken with the owner

- **Operator accounts can recover a password.** `/account/auth/callback`
  exchanges the confirmation and reset links (previously they fell through to
  the Site URL with an unexchanged code), `/account/auth/update-password`
  sets the new password, and the sign-in panel offers "forgot your password"
  and translated errors instead of Supabase's raw wording. The control
  project needs `…/account/auth/callback` in its redirect URLs (README).
- **Signing out of a department ends this browser's session only**
  (`scope: "local"`), not the member's sessions on every device.
- **A department's legal pages are indexed only when the department is
  public.** An indexed imprint put unlisted departments into search results.
- **The abuse queue can no longer be packed and says when it is full.**
  `report_department()` limits each sender to five reports an hour, keyed on
  a SHA-256 digest of the client address PostgREST passes in
  `request.headers` and, separately, of the contact address; a full queue
  raises `QUEUE_FULL` and the form tells the reporter where to write instead.
  Needs `db/control-plane.sql` re-run on the control plane.
- Titles use " · " as their separator; HTML responses send
  `Vary: Cookie, Accept-Language`. German stays unindexed by choice: one
  address per page, language by cookie.
- Next stays on 15.x. The remaining PostCSS advisory is inside Next's own
  build-time copy and needs Next 16 to clear.
